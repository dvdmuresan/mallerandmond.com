      Why we joined forces

      Our briefs kept getting bigger: festivals, cultural institutions, identities that have to work on a stage, a street and a screen at the same time. Rather than turning that work down or stretching a small team thin, we put the two studios side by side. STUDIO MALLER brings strategy-led branding and space design. MOND : STUDIO brings identity, art direction and motion. Clients get one team, one point of contact and both studios’ full attention.

      Double D’s, double trouble.

      There’s a David in each studio. Call for David in a meeting and two people answer. We count that as twice the attention for the same brief.
